Revenue uncertainty

The observed revenue gain was $0.93 per eligible user. The Welch 95% CI was $0.55–$1.32. A 5,000-resample bootstrap produced $0.56–$1.34.

Methodology

What the data
can tell us.

These comparisons describe the selected cohorts under the 72-hour analysis. The assignment mechanism needs independent verification before interpreting the lift as causal.

Simulated experiment
The dataset comes from Oscar Palomo’s checkout A/B test project. A real checkout experiment would need its own validation.
Protocol and eligibility
The 72-hour window and exclusions for checkout_one_page_v1 belong to the extended analysis. The original randomization and intended allocation require verification.
Funnel coverage
The dataset supports assignment-to-order conversion. Product views, cart events and payment-step events are absent.
Refund interpretation
The refund flag does not establish timing, partial versus full refunds, or whether recorded revenue already reflects a refund.
Guardrails
Average order value and refund-rate differences are descriptive. The revenue significance test uses revenue per eligible user.
